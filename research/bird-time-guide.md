# Bird Time (Feed the Pale Raven) Guide

Use this when you're at the Perch in Dagsion and want Perfect Bird Time instead of guessing.

## How it works

- Unlocks in **Chapter 4**. Once per in-game week, no Turn cost. Resets Sunday.
- Pick a **recruited** character. They say 3 lines drawn at random from their pool of about 10. You answer each with a bird action.
- A right answer shows **"They seem happy."** Get all 3 right for **Perfect Bird Time**, a bigger Support boost with Eshmel.
- Wrong answers still give a little Support.
- No timer on answers. The game's only hint is the line itself: match its tone (intimidating to boasts, calm to quiet lines). Some right answers are very unintuitive.

## Why it matters: Support Salvation

Higher Support with Eshmel raises the chance of **Support Salvation**, which works in Part I:

- **Battle maps:** Eshmel blocks an attack that would have killed that unit.
- **Clashes:** Eshmel fires Flying Bright Spear and wipes the enemy party when yours is near death.

On Hard (and especially Classic), give Bird Time to your frontliners and your most fragile key units first.

## Other characters

Line-by-line answers for the rest of the cast weren't readable during research (Game8 and GameRant blocked fetching). Options:

- Open [Game8's Pale Raven answers](https://game8.co/games/Fire-Emblem-Fortunes-Weave/archives/623686) or [KeenGamer's list](https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/fire-emblem-fortunes-weave-perfect-bird-time-answers-for-every-character/) on your phone.
- Or tell me the character and the exact line on screen, and I'll look it up.
- Record right answers here as you find them.

Useful pattern from KeenGamer's answer pools: most characters' right answers are mostly **Nod, Tilt your head, Spread your wings, Gaze intently** and **Sing a beautiful tune**. Rare actions appear only for certain characters, such as **Dance** (Leda, Esmeralda), **Sing softly** (Catania, Halvin, Io, Kiroc, others), **Do a somersault**, **Cling to your perch** (Fianna) and **Tremble** (Goliath). If you see a rare option, it's often the answer for that character's odd line (unverified heuristic).

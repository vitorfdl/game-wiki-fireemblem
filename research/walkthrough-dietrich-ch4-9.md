# Dietrich's Path, Chapters 4 to 9

Your play plan from the first Free Time (3/27) to the end of Chapter 9 (9/19). That's six chapters and most of Dietrich's path. Come back after the Battle at Kunlun for Chapters 10 to 12.

**Dates** are month/day (RPG Site format): 9/17 is 17 September.

**Tags**
- **[MISSABLE]** gone for this path if you skip it
- **[DEADLINE m/d]** do it by that date
- **[CARRIES]** unlocks something that helps your later paths too
- **[EXTRA]** a second copy of a unit whose home is another route, recruited here to fill Part II slots

## Our route decisions

**Play order: Dietrich first, then Cai, Theodora, Leda**

This path's job, beyond its own story:

1. **Unlock Guardian and Ranger for later paths [CARRIES].**
   - Beat the Il-Lara challenge (Renown 6, Ch 8) and the Oko challenge (Renown 10, late Ch 11 at best, often Ch 12).
   - Jester takes Ranger in Part II Ch 1. Guardian has no user here: Esmeralda plays armored instead.
   - Cai's healers and Leda's tank want Guardian; Cai and Theodora want Ranger.
   - Unlocks only carry over once you finish all 12 chapters and start the next path from Chapter 1.
2. **Unlock the Blacksmith class [CARRIES].**
   - It takes 10 upgrades to 10 different Combat Arts at Smyrnos's Temple (Temple Reception > Boost Combat Arts).
   - Cost: AllThings.How says the cheapest cost 20 Magic Echo Shards each, so 200 in total; RPG Site estimates about 400.
   - Farm Answerer's Cry the whole way.
3. **Bank Karma Shards.**
   - Clearing this path pays about 5,000, and Dietrich has 8 Paralogues, the most of any lord.
   - Your first Boons will come from this.
4. **Explore the desert.**
   - Map exploration is shared across the save, and Cai can't enter the desert at all in Part I.

## Every week, from Chapter 4 on

None of these cost a Turn, and they all reset on Sunday. The clock only moves when you take a Turn-costing action, so do all of these first.

- [ ] **Inn meals** with Dietrich and whoever you're raising Support with.
  - One meal raises several Supports at once, so it's the main Support tool.
- [ ] **Gifts and meals:** follow the Supports section below.
  - Short version: every Sunday hold 3 meals (Dietrich + 2 targets), and give each target one gift that week.
- [ ] **God audiences:** take one whenever a temple shows **gold** on the map.
  - That isn't every week, so check the map each day.
  - Each gives about 50 Renown, no Turn cost (Renown 2 or higher).
  - Good when it shows up, but you can't plan Renown around it. On this path Renown is the bottleneck.
- [ ] **Divine Services at Temple Row: serve all six gods, each on its own day.**
  - Each god can be served once a week at no Turn cost.
  - Two units spend 1 Motivation each; serving on the god's day gives a guaranteed Perfect that refunds the Motivation, so on-day service is free.
  - Bless levels are shared across every path.

| Day | God | Lv 1 Blessing | Why it matters on this path |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Aurora | Halves physical damage | Safety on Hard |
| Tuesday | Mars | Physical Hit +50 | The main fix for missing on Hard |
| Wednesday | Smyrnos | Magic Hit +50 | **Smyrnos Lv 1 is a Blacksmith unlock condition** (GameRant) |
| Thursday | Jurah | Negates effective damage | Cheapest Blessing to cast |
| Friday | Kalla | Avoid +50 | Lv 3 gives Mov +2 |
| Saturday | Credna | +5 vision in fog | Theodora's Paralogue in Ch 9 is fogged |

**Divine Services priority (if you only have time for some)** [FOLD]
- Order: Aurora, Kalla, Smyrnos, Mars, then Jurah and Credna.
- A Part II video ranks the Blessings the same way:
  - Aurora: cheap, halves damage for a turn.
  - Kalla up to Lv 3: Avoid +50, Mov +2.
  - Smyrnos up to Lv 2: magic Rng +1 lets Fabio and Olympia poke 2x2 armored units without a counter.
  - Credna is the weakest in Part II, so take only Lv 1 for the fogged Ch 9 Paralogue.
- **Service also raises Support between the two units you send** (Game8): always send Dietrich plus the army member whose Support you want most that week.
- You can equip four Blessings in battle. Each costs Divine Sand, which refills every story battle.

## Supports: gifts and meals, week by week

### How it works (confirmed rules)
- **Meals** are at the **Inn in Dagsion**.
  - You get **3 meals a week**: the menu has a few dishes, and each can be ordered once a week.
  - The menu resets on **Sunday**.
- **3 seats per meal**, and every pair at the table gains Support.
  - Recruit Support only counts **with Dietrich**, so the setup is always **Dietrich + 2 targets**.
  - A meal without Dietrich wastes the recruit Support.
- **Price:** 300G per meal, **150G on Sunday**. Hold all three meals on Sunday. Meals cost no Turn.
- **People who haven't joined can come to meals** once you've given them one gift. That's how you build recruit Support before they join.
- **Pick a dish all three like.**
  - Each diner shows an icon on the dish: two notes means loves, one note means likes, an angry icon means dislikes.
  - If all three at least like it, you get a **Perfect** meal (bigger gain).
  - A liked dish also restores 2 Motivation instead of 1, so do the Arena and Temple first, then eat.
- **Gifts:** one per character per week, no Turn cost.
  - A **loved** gift (two gold arrows) gives about double.
  - **The first gift only unlocks the bond** (Game8), so make it a 27G pastry and save good gifts for later weeks.
- **Finding people:** open **Map > People**. It shows who is in Dagsion right now and your Support with them, and it lets you teleport to them.
- **Speed guide:** one player reached Support 2 in a single Sunday of three Perfect meals. Support 3 takes several weeks, so start early.

### Your weekly routine
1. **Sunday:** hold all 3 meals at half price, using the chapter's meal plan below.
2. **Any day that week:** give each current target their best gift from the shopping list below.
3. Check Map > People. When a target reaches the level they need, drop them from meals and move the next person in.

### Meal plan by chapter

**How to use it:** find your chapter below. Each row is one meal: seat those three, then order the **first dish in the list that's on this week's menu**. Each dish can only be ordered once a week, so the bold first picks never repeat within a chapter.

**When none of the listed dishes is on the menu**
1. **Hold the meal anyway.** Who sits matters more than the dish. A plain meal still raises Support for all three pairs; a Perfect one raises it more.
2. Use the full dish table below. Pick the dish with the most notes for the two targets and no angry icon for anyone.
3. Only if every dish on the menu upsets one of the targets, seat a different target from the same chapter instead.

**Why some dishes never show up as Perfect**
- Dietrich is neutral on Salt-Crusted Ham, Sand Boar and Beans, Sweet-and-Sour Stick Bugs, Charred Fish Grill, Fish-Sauce Soup and Garlic Seared Scallops.
- A Perfect meal needs all three diners to like the dish, so those six can't be Perfect with Dietrich at the table.
- They're still fine fallbacks when both targets love them (Salt-Crusted Ham for Fabio, Mikaela, Kiroc, Goliath or Io).

**Part II changes how you finish Part I**

Part II has no free time, no shops and no recruiting between battles. Plan Ch 10 to 12 around that:

- **Anna's Paralogue in Ch 10 opens Anna's shop** (north of Temple Row, rotating stock).
  - The video says a Ch 10 shop sells a **Giant's Ring**, but no written source lists it there.
  - One reports it as a Vandahl barter in Alecto. Check both.
- **Ch 12 is your last shopping trip.**
  - Only what a unit holds at the end of Ch 12 goes into Part II. The game says "equipped"; we read it as the unit's 6 items, consumables included?
  - The rest waits in storage until Part III. The per-unit list is in the Chapters 10 to 12 guide.
- **Don't hoard weapons.**
  - Part II repairs every weapon at the start of each chapter and hands out Iron +3 weapons for the whole army.
  - Use Combat Arts freely in Ch 12, even if it breaks weapons.
- **Army training catches up late joiners.**
  - Before each of the first four Part II battles, pick Army Training at the Dispatch Officer.
  - Anyone below the floor jumps to Lv 34, 35, 37, then 39, and gains stats from **the class they're in**.
  - From the Lv 35 floor (Ch 2), everyone can take Advanced exams, and Part II gives 5 Advanced Licenses per chapter: Dante, Simon and Jester (plus Olympia or Goliath if they missed Part I).

**Class to hold before Army Training**

| Unit | Class | Why |

| Simon | Brigand | Str |
| Dante | Priest | Res |
| Olympia | Shaman, if she isn't an Ovate yet | Mag |

- **Recruit everyone you can.** Army training makes a late or unused recruit as useful as a core unit, so bench depth is free.

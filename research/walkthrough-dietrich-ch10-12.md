# Dietrich's Path, Chapters 10 to 12

Your plan from the start of Chapter 10 (9/29) to the end of Part I (11/27), then into Part II. It picks up where the Chapters 4 to 9 guide stops.

**Where you are**
- About Renown 8. The Renown pace table puts you at 8 to 9 during Ch 10 (one tracked game: Renown 8, 180 short of 9). If you're still at 7, Anna's Paralogue and Its Name Is Da Mina are the quickest fixes.
- Ursula recruited.
- Dante, Catania and Gaitz still to recruit.
- Yang Jie, Fabio, Dietrich and Esmeralda near Lv 35; Mikaela and Kiroc around Lv 30; Olympia and Goliath in their 20s.
- Player data from a full clear: Dietrich ends Part I around Lv 38, most deployed units Lv 30 to 35, and 7 to 8 units reach Advanced.

**Sources:** RPG Site first, Game8 for gaps. Anything from one source only is marked.

**Tags**
- **[MISSABLE]** gone for this path if you skip it
- **[DEADLINE m/d]** do it by that date
- **[CARRIES]** unlocks something for later paths
- **[PART II]** matters for what you bring into Part II

## The three goals for these chapters

1. **Renown 10 before 11/27.**
   - It gates **Gaitz** (Ch 12) and **Oko**, who unlocks **Ranger** for Jester and for later paths.
   - You need all of Renown 9 and all of Renown 10, so take every Renown source below.
   - On a real Dietrich clear, Renown 10 arrived at the start of Ch 12 and Renown 12 by the end.
2. **Advanced classes for 8 units**, in this order: Yang Jie, Fabio, Dietrich, Esmeralda, Mikaela, Kiroc, Olympia, Goliath. Renown 8 opens them, usually early in Ch 10; the last four land in Ch 11 to 12 as they reach Lv 35. Everyone else (Dante, Simon, Jester) takes Advanced in Part II Ch 1 to 2 on the free Licenses.
3. **Get ready for Part II.**
   - It has no Free Time, no shops and no recruiting.
   - Ch 12 is your last chance for all of that.

### The Thermae (Renown 8, usually Ch 10)
- Pick **two recruited units** to bathe together.
  - It restores HP, magic uses and Motivation.
  - It raises Support between the two.
- Each unit gets **one random temporary Ability** from that bath's pool.
  - It lasts until the end of the chapter and takes an Ability slot (5 slots from Specialty on).
  - If the slots are full, equip it by hand from the Learned list.
- **The "special" baths** are the Sunday-only **Special Baths**, which hold the stronger "+" abilities.
  - Treat it as a chapter buff, not a skill teacher: no permanent or character-unique Thermae skills are known.
- Cost: 500G per bath (Game8, single source).
- Renown 9 (usually Ch 11) adds three more baths.

**Best use here:** a Sunday Special Bath in the last week before each main battle, so the buff lasts through it.

| Bath (Sunday) | Pool includes | Give it to |
|---|---|---|
| Giants' Bath | Crit Effect+, Str Effect+, Absorption+, Arts Effect+ | **Dietrich** before Mars (he duels alone) |
| Sea-Tyrant's Bath | HP Effect+, Def Effect+, Revitalize+, Fury | Dietrich or a tank (Esmeralda, Goliath) |
| Unicorns' Bath | Spd, Avo, Hit, Mov Effect+ | Catania before the Bertrand battle |
| Harpyrian Bath | Heal Effect+, Mag Effect+, Max Healing, Fast Magic | Yang Jie, Dante, Olympia |

    - Right: bows and an armored tank.
    - Left: mages, Catania, Dietrich and Esmeralda.
  - Green tiles heal.
  - Three chokepoints hold Mars in; he has no ranged attack.
  - **Never hit The Brandisher (Mars's beast form) with artillery.** It summons reinforcements.
- **Part 2: Dietrich fights Mars alone (everyone else is pushed back)**
  - Bring **Answerer Arisen** (or your best sword).
  - ⚠️ A 4-weight sword (Answerer Arisen, Silver or Da Mina) + Gale Ring (2) = 6, inside his budget; Killing Edge (6) + ring = 8. Leave the Hunter's Ring (4), Levin Sword (8) and Zanbato (7) in the bag for the duel.
  - Duel kit, 6/6: Answerer Arisen, Gale Ring, Elixir, Concoction, Dew of Protection, Dew of Peace. Consumables share the 6 slots, so hand the Silver Sword to Jester and leave the Killing Edge in storage.
  - Bring his best Combat Arts with boosts.
  - Use the heal tiles.
  - The Sunday before: **Giants' Bath** (Crit, Str) or **Sea-Tyrant's Bath** (HP, Def) on Dietrich.
  - Equip Aurora's Blessing (halves damage for a turn) for the duel.
- **Deploy:** Dietrich, Esmeralda, Goliath, Kiroc, Ursula, Fabio, Olympia, Yang Jie, Dante, Catania.
- Pack: Dietrich both Dews; Esmeralda a Concoction in the Pilum's slot; Mikaela an Elixir, Hammer to storage. Their kits stay as the Part II stock.

**Before you move on:** Gaitz recruited, and everything in the Part II checklist below.

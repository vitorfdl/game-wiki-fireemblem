# Part I Walkthrough: Dietrich's Path

A chapter-by-chapter plan for Dietrich's route. It tells you what to do in each Free Time: who to recruit, which exams to take, which challenges and subquests to clear, and what you can miss. For objectives and map details, keep RPG Site's Dietrich path guide open next to this.

**Date format:** all dates are month/day, as in RPG Site and the path guide. 9/17 means 17 September. (fortunesweave.co.uk writes day/month.)

**Chapter estimates** such as "Renown 6 (usually Ch 8)" come from the Renown pace table below, which is built from a real Dietrich clear (about Renown 10 at the start of Ch 12). The real gate is always the Renown number, so check your Renown screen.

### Renown pace

What you can count on by the end of each Free Time on Dietrich's path. Dietrich's main Renown source is the **Dread Warrior Challenges** (150R each). Each one appears at its own Renown level, from Borges (Renown 2) to Oko (Renown 10). Add 8 Paralogues (150R each).

**Catching up**
- Clear each Dread Warrior Challenge in the chapter it appears. Leaving them all for 11/27 is what makes the Ch 10 to 12 gates slip.
- Take a god audience (about +50) every time a temple shows gold on the map.
- Pick **For the Empire's Safety** in Ch 8 (200R) and grab the 100R quests: Garum, Garum, Garum! (Ch 8) and Its Name Is Da Mina (Ch 10).
- The Ch 9 Paralogues (4 × 150R) are the biggest jump. Don't skip Leda's or Theodora's.

### The decisions that matter most

1. **Bertrand's Paralogue on 9/17, the only day it runs.** It's the only way to get Gaitz. Save on 9/16.
2. **Talimun's Paralogue for Ursula.** Do it on 9/18 or 9/19. The fallback window is 10/1 to 10/10.
3. **Start Support early.**
   - Five recruits need Support 3: Jester, Goliath, Simon, Ursula, Gaitz. Dante needs Support 2.
   - Give each one a 27G pastry (Lowtown Tavern) to unlock meals.
   - Then seat two of them with Dietrich at each of the 3 Sunday meals.
   - The exact meal plan, dishes and gift list are in the Field Kit.
4. **Push Renown.**
   - You need Renown 8 for Dante and Advanced exams (usually Ch 10), and Renown 10 for Gaitz (Ch 12).
   - Every Dread Warrior Challenge and Paralogue gives 150R.
   - God audiences give about 50R when a temple shows gold on the map (not every week).
5. **Chapter 8 Sealed Letter choice.** You can only do one per Path.
   - "For the Empire's Safety" pays a Specialty License and 200R now.
   - "A Letter of Some Gravity" starts a chain that ends in the Haze Badge.

### Weekly chores (no Turn cost)

Do these every week from Ch 4 on. They reset on Sunday.

- [ ] Sunday: 3 Inn meals, each Dietrich + 2 targets (150G on Sunday instead of 300G).
  - Pick a dish all three like for a Perfect meal.
- [ ] One gift per target: a 27G pastry the first time, then their liked gift.
  - Gift list: see the Field Kit.
- [ ] Arena: one session a week, two units. Pairs are on the Arena sheet (A).
- [ ] Divine Services at Temple Row
- [ ] Feed the Pale Raven (Dietrich's answers: see the Field Kit)
- [ ] God audiences for Renown when a temple shows gold on the map (need Renown 2, usually late Ch 4; not every week)

**Send Supplies does not exist on this route.** That's Theodora's system. Dietrich's route quests are the Dread Warrior Challenges, which all end 11/27.

## Chapter 1: A Stranger Comes to Dagda

No Free Time.

**Story battle: Tutorial vs. Bandits**

- Win: defeat all enemies. Lose if Dietrich or Fabio falls.
- Learn the controls and let Fabio heal.

**Before you move on:** nothing to miss.

## Chapter 2: Sacrifice and Strife

No Free Time. Fishing town of Fina.

**Story battle: Sea Beasts vs. Redears**

- Win: defeat Redears. Lose if Dietrich or Esmeralda falls.
- Esmeralda joins.
- Spread EXP across all three units.
- Fortuna's Blessing unlocks on turn 2.
- Have Dietrich or Fabio talk to Esmeralda for Support.
- Grab the yellow orbs for random common items.

**Before you move on:** nothing to miss.

## Chapter 3: The Grand Megalith

No Free Time.

**Heretics' Cave (pick a 5-member party)**

- Y is a preemptive strike, ZR shows the path.
- Loot the Dew of Protection and Vulnerary.

**Story battle: vs. Saint Katarina**

- The objective changes to "Defeat Anatolia" partway through. Lose if Dietrich falls.
- Sand lowers Avo.
- Dietrich and Esmeralda hold the line. Keep Fabio behind them.

**Before you move on:** nothing to miss.

## Open questions in the sources

- **Jester's 3 quests:** RPG Site only says "3x Quest". No details.
- **Earliest Giant's Meat access:** the desert shows up in the Ch 8 to-do list, and the Giant skirmishes are around Lv 35. Goliath may join later than Renown 6 alone suggests.
- **Where recruits stand in Dagsion:** use Map > People, which lists who is in Dagsion and teleports you to them.
- **Mature Blush shop:** not named.
- **Renown per chapter:** the Renown pace table comes from one player's clear (Renown 10 at the start of Ch 12, 12 at the end). Your pace can differ by a level either way.

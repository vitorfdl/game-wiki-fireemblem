# Fortune's Weave Part I Walkthrough: Cai's Route

A chapter-by-chapter plan for Cai's Part I route.

- It follows the route roster plan: 12 units, and nobody from another route's plan.
- It also recruits three Part II extras: second copies of Sirocco, Fianna and Mikaela, whose home copies belong to other routes.
- Battle and quest details come from RPG Site's Cai path guide.
- Builds follow class paths built from the class and weapon guide, the growth tables and the Hard-mode videos.
- Those paths add side trips (detours) in the waiting windows between exams.

**Carried-in classes**
- Cai's path is the second one you play, after Dietrich's.
- From Dietrich's path: Guardian (Ninae), Ranger (Peppe) and Blacksmith (no user).
- Cai's own mentorships add Caladrius (Renown 7, usually late Ch 8, Cai), Dragoon (Renown 8, usually Ch 10; Seteth in Ch 11 or 12, Nydine in Part II) and Troubadour (Renown 10, usually Ch 11, Fianna).
- Cataphract and Dancer aren't available here.

**Mounts**
- Only Cai's route has the Stable. Every pick, bait and spawn tile is in the Mounts tab (key T).
- A ridden mount adds its growths only while the unit is mounted.
- Every mount must be in the Stable by the end of Ch 12, bench riders' and Part II side trips' included.

**Date format:** all dates are month/day, the format RPG Site's path guide uses. So 9/17 means 17 September.

**How to catch up**
- Do every Kindness Quest (100 to 250 each) in the chapter it appears. Deliver the three crop quests the day the crops are ready, not on 11/27.
- Take a god audience whenever a temple shows gold on the map (about +50, no Turn).
- Clear the Ch 10 paralogues early (150 each). "For the Empire's Safety" (Ch 8) pays 200.
- Generic subquests you finished on Dietrich's route complete themselves here, so Kindness Quests carry most of your Renown.

**Side trips (detours)**
- Once certified, a unit switches between its classes for free at the Proctor, the Base Menu or Battle Prep. A side trip costs one License, not the old class.
- Early units master their Specialty class around Ch 7, then wait for Lv 35 and Renown 8 (usually Ch 10). That wait is Ch 7 to 9.
- This plan spends that wait in a second class for a mastery, a stat fix or an exam rank, then switches back or moves on.
- Mastery speed is undocumented, so every switch waits on a condition ("once Regroup is mastered"), not a fixed chapter.
- The Increase Class EXP boon speeds all of them.

**Quest types**
- Cai's route quests are Kindness Quests and Mentorships.
- Send Supplies belongs to Theodora's route and doesn't exist here.
- Generic subquests show up semi-randomly, so treat the subquest notes below as "if offered".

**Why this core**
- The old plan made Ultand a Guardian. That put three healers (Tialla, Ultand, Ninae) in eight slots and left Cai as the only caster until Sirocco.
- Ultand has no Physic, and the Hard video names Ovate as a good class for her. She now goes Diviner, Shaman, Ovate: two Physic healers and two casters from late Ch 7.
- Noctula keeps her slot on merit, not just her EXP lead: she rates B, above every other early option (Majide D, Nydine C), and she is the only dodge tank.
- Sirocco joins late (Renown 9, late Ch 10 or Ch 11) and still deploys because he replaces Ultand as the main nuke (S tier against her A/B).
- Majide, Nydine, Jasmine, Mikaela and Peppe fill no missing role, so they wait for Part II. Fianna only borrows the Ch 10 ninth slot and dungeon runs for a shot at Troubadour.

**Synergies to set up on the map**
- **Dodge-tank pocket (Noctula, Ultand, Ninae)**
  - Noctula baits from a forest or thicket.
  - Ultand ends beside her: Pray for Safety adds Ddg +10 (+20 at Lv 35), and Patch Up heals her after every enemy-phase fight.
  - Ninae takes the other side, so Spirits' Guidance gives Noctula Hit/Avo +10 against mages (her Res grows 20%).
- **Lend Magic at range 2 (Tialla on Cai, Ultand, Sirocco)**
  - Tactician's Wit adds range to Tialla's Lend arts.
  - Lend Magic (Authority C) the caster before it nukes.
  - Lend Strength+ (Authority B) goes on Guzran.
- **Charge lane (Seteth, Peter)**
  - Seteth plugs the choke on enemy phase (Ready for Battle, Sense Threat, Silver Shield + Gale Ring: 6 extra weight, so he isn't doubled).
  - Next turn Peter Charges down the line of foes stacked in front of him.
  - Charger adds Prt +10 because the chariot can't dodge.
- **Crescendo feed (Peter, with Guzran and Cai)**
  - Leave targets low so Peter takes the early kills.
  - Crescendo stacks Hit +3 per kill (up to +30) before he swings the Hit -30 Deadeye Longbow.
- **Two Physic users (Tialla, Ninae)**
  - Tialla heals from the back, Ninae from the front.
  - They carry Ch 9 alone while Ultand is out.
- **Mounted strike team (Cai, Peter, Seteth)**
  - Paired Run on bonded mounts, Peter's Canto, and Cai's Brio and Overtake let the three hit a flank and pull back out of reach.
- **Underworld maps (Cai, Guzran)**
  - Flamekeeper makes every ally immune to Underworld Flames and halves damage taken in them.
  - Guzran's Cleansing Blade ignores the Underworld boon.

**Catch the exact mount you want** [FOLD]
1. Hard save on the animal tile, before you set the lure.
2. Lure, spend the Turns elsewhere, come back and Capture.
3. Check the breed, paired ability and color.
4. Not the one? System > Return to Title, then load the save.
5. Walk to another node and back before luring again. The same moves give the same roll.

- Roll for Mov (Paired Run, Paired Streak) or Canto. Skip Paired Alertness.
- Out of days? Restarting the chapter buys more free time.

### The decisions that matter most

1. **Do Aurora's "Special Request from Mom" at Renown 8 (usually Ch 10).**
   - It pays 8,000G and unlocks Dragoon for Seteth (Ch 11 or 12) and Nydine (Part II Ch 2), your only two fliers.
   - Do it the day Renown 8 lands: Seteth's exam depends on it, and Renown doesn't carry into Part II.
   - Part II needs 2 or 3 fliers (Ch 2 has a fleeing Ranger to catch).
2. **Do Bertrand's "The White-Maned Demon's Request" at Renown 10 (usually Ch 11; due 11/27).**
   - Game8 and Game Rant say it unlocks Troubadour, which Fianna needs. RPG Site lists only an Advanced License.
   - If Troubadour doesn't show up, keep Fianna in Priest.

## Supports: gifts and meals, week by week

### How it works (confirmed rules)
- **Meals** are at the **Inn in Dagsion**. You get **3 meals a week**.
  - The menu has a few dishes, and each can be ordered once a week. The menu resets on **Sunday**.
- **3 seats per meal**, and every pair at the table gains Support.
  - Recruit Support only counts **with Cai**, so the setup is always **Cai + 2 targets**.
  - A meal without Cai wastes the recruit Support.
- **Price:** 300G per meal, **150G on Sunday**. Hold all three meals on Sunday (450G a week). Meals cost no Turn.
- **People who haven't joined can come to meals** once you've given them one gift. Give the gift before you sit down.
- **Pick a dish all three like.**
  - Each diner shows an icon on the dish: two notes means loves, one note means likes, an angry icon means dislikes.
  - If all three at least like it, you get a **Perfect** meal (bigger gain).
  - A liked dish also restores 2 Motivation instead of 1, so do the Arena and Temple first, then eat.
- **No dish can be named for Cai's meals.**
  - The only dish data comes from one player's Dietrich game and doesn't cover Cai.
  - Every week, pick the dish where Cai and both targets show a note.
  - Mikaela is the only target in that data. She loves Salt-Crusted Ham, Sand Boar and Beans, Sweet-and-Sour Stick Bugs and Charred Fish Grill.
  - She dislikes Madazins and Cheese-Coated Crayfish.
- **Gifts:** one per character per week, no Turn cost.
  - A **loved** gift (two gold arrows) gives about double.
  - **The first gift only unlocks the bond** (Game8), so make it a 27G pastry and save good gifts for later weeks.
- **Finding people:** open **Map > People**. It shows who is in Dagsion right now and your Support with them, and it lets you teleport to them.
- **Speed guide:** one player reached Support 2 in a single Sunday of three Perfect meals. Support 3 takes several weeks, so start early.
- **Meal upgrade:** players report a bigger meal rank ("meal time level 3") in Cai's Ch 10. What triggers it is unknown.

### Your weekly routine
1. **Sunday:** hold all 3 meals at half price, using the chapter's meal plan below.
2. **Any day that week:** give each current target their weekly gift from the shopping list below.
3. Check Map > People. When a target reaches the level they need, drop them from meals and move the next person in.
4. The first week Map > People shows **Mikaela, Fianna or Sirocco**, give them a 27G pastry so they can eat that Sunday.

## Part II changes how you finish Part I

Part II has no free time, no shops and no recruiting between battles. Plan Ch 10 to 12 around that:

- **A Ch 10 Paralogue opens an accessory shop** whose stock changes each chapter. Buy a **Giant's Ring** as soon as it opens.
  - The video says this Paralogue exists on every path except Leda's but doesn't name it.
  - On Dietrich's path it's Anna's, so on Cai's it's most likely **Anna's** (Golden Secret).
  - Bertrand's and Talimun's also skip Leda's path, so check the shops after each of the three.
- **Ch 12 is your last shopping trip.** What a unit holds at the end of Ch 12 (6 items?) is its whole Part II kit, so stock Elixirs and Concoctions now (Part II Ch 3 asks for plenty). Everything else waits in storage until Part III.
- **Don't hoard weapons.** Part II repairs every weapon at the start of each chapter and hands out Iron +3 weapons for the whole army.
  - Use Combat Arts freely in Ch 12, even if it breaks weapons.
- **Army Training catches up late joiners.** Before each of the first four Part II battles, pick Army Training at the Dispatch Officer.

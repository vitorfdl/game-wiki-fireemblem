**Why the detours**
- Seven units are here by Ch 5, and Renown 8 doesn't come until Ch 10. Most of them master their Specialty class long before Advanced exams open.
- Each detour buys a rank the next exam needs, or a mastery the role uses.
- Certified classes stay switchable for free, so a detour costs one License, not the old class.
- Mastery speed varies. Leave a detour when its exit condition (listed with each detour) is met, not on a fixed chapter, and cut it if the main step would slip.

### Route mechanics and your builds

Leda's route runs on Perform Quests, Drink Recipes, the Dancer class and her Songs. Each one shapes the builds.

**Perform Quests check no stat**
- Leda travels to the town and performs. The quest pays Renown (150 to 200), gold, crates and Leaves.
- No stat check, minigame or second unit appears in any source (RPG Site, KeenGamer, fortunesweave.online), so nobody trains Cha for them.
- Their job is Renown: Specialty exams in Ch 6, Advanced and Sha Lan (Renown 8) in Ch 10, Song of Angels (Renown 10) late Ch 11 or Ch 12.

**Dancer shapes the army**
- The Necklace comes from Great Dancer's Successor (Hingosh to Baina Oasis, Ch 9).
- Dancer needs Bow B and Sword B, which is why Leda passes through Rogue and Myrmidon.
- Sirocco, Inyoni and Guzran deploy partly because they are the best units to dance. Ludia would be a fourth target in Part II.

**Watch Leda's Blaze Gauge**
- Each Song adds 2 (Madness 3, Angels 4).
- Half full is Overblaze. Full is Burst, which strips bonuses for the map and cuts max HP, and Leda is the defeat condition.
- Underworld Madness sets the gauge to 6 and locks Overblaze. Improviso (Lv 35, her personal skill, always on) then lets her attack after a Song. It never adds a second Dance.

**Song of Angels (once per map)**
- Every ally adjacent to Leda acts again.
- Save it for the Ch 12 boss turn with Sirocco, Inyoni, Guzran and Esmeralda around her. Ch 11 only if Renown 10 came early.

## Supports: gifts and meals, week by week

### How it works (confirmed rules)
- **Meals** are at the **Inn in Dagsion**. You get **3 meals a week**.
  - The menu has a few dishes, and each can be ordered once a week. The menu resets on **Sunday**.
- **3 seats per meal**, and every pair at the table gains Support.
  - Recruit Support only counts **with Leda**, so the setup is always **Leda + 2 targets**.
  - A meal without Leda wastes the recruit Support.
- **Price:** 300G per meal, **150G on Sunday**. Hold all three meals on Sunday. Meals cost no Turn.
  - If a free time ends before its last Sunday, eat on the last day at 300G rather than skip the week.
- **People who haven't joined can come to meals** once you've given them one gift. That's how you build recruit Support before they join.
- **Pick a dish all three like.** If all three at least like it, you get a **Perfect** meal (bigger gain).
  - Icons on the dish: two notes means loves, one note means likes, an angry icon means dislikes.
  - Leda likes 9 of the 12 recorded dishes and dislikes Goat Roast, Madazins and Cheese-Coated Crayfish (`dishes.json`; Field Kit › Meals › Which dish).
  - A liked dish also restores 2 Motivation instead of 1, so do the Arena and Temple first, then eat.
- **Gifts:** one per character per week, no Turn cost. A **loved** gift (two gold arrows) gives about double.
  - **The first gift only unlocks the bond** (Game8), so make it the cheapest liked item and save good gifts for later weeks.
- **Finding people:** open **Map > People**. It shows who is in Dagsion right now and your Support with them, and it lets you teleport to them.
  - If a target isn't in Dagsion, give the seat to the next name in the queue below.
- **Speed guide:** one player reached Support 2 in a single Sunday of three Perfect meals. Support 3 takes several weeks, so start early.

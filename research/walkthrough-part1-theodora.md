**Why Tobias goes Pugilist, Brigand, then Warrior.** [FOLD]

- A player who cleared Parts I and II on Hard rates Tobias low A: he hits hard but misses a lot.
- That video uses him as the example for Warrior's mastery, Practiced Art (Hit +10 when attacking with a Combat Art). With an accurate axe and an art, his Hit goes from about 30% to about 60%.
- Warrior also gives Axe Hit +5, Combat Arts +2 and Mov 6, and every art he uses triggers his Power Arts too.
- Warrior needs Gauntlet B. Arena Warrior Training raises it, but Pugilist adds more, so his Specialty is Pugilist instead of Brigand:
  - It trains Gauntlet +2 in battle.
  - It grows more Spd and Def (Spd 35, Def 55 on him against Brigand's 30 and 50).
  - Its mastery Attack, Get Back (Avo +10 when using an art) fits a unit that always attacks with arts.
- Dreadnought's Hit bonus only works on enemy phase, and the video warns it has very low Res and Mov 4.
- Cataphract rates C and grows worse than Warrior on him.
- If you want a second armored wall instead, run Soldier, Armored Knight, Dreadnought.
- He should master Pugilist well before Lv 35 (mastery pace is undocumented), so he spends Ch 8 to 9 in Brigand:
  - Regroup (Avo +20 when a foe dodges his attack) answers his misses.
  - Brigand trains Axe +2 a fight toward Warrior's Axe B.
  - Its Str 75 growth beats Pugilist's 70.

**Why Theodora detours through Priest.** [FOLD]

- Soldier and Armored Knight list Spear, Sword and Axe only, so she can't cast Heal or Seraphim in either.
- Priest (Ch 8 to 9, White Magic C) is her first class with White Magic: doubled heals, and White Magic +2 a fight.
- That way Physic (White Magic B, single source) is ready the day she becomes a Guardian.
- The cost is about 2 Def over 7 levels (Priest Def growth 30 against Armored Knight's 65).
- She fights with an axe from the second row for two chapters.

**Why Loretta goes Myrmidon.** [FOLD]

- Sword boon and Spd 55: Myrmidon (Lv 20) and Shido (Lv 35) double and dodge, and Sword S opens The Blade in Part III (D-130).
- Steadfast (Avo +10 at half HP) and Peak Form (Atk +3 at full HP) want her topped up: Sofia's range-3 heals, Ultand adjacent.

**Hard-mode class notes.** [FOLD]

- Guardian and Bishop are the video's top classes, so Theodora and Sofia come first for kills.
- Healing is mandatory on Hard from Part II. Until Theodora's Priest detour (Ch 8), Bonaventure and Sofia are your only healers.
- Dragoons get one-shot by archers, so check bow range before Lysander moves.
- Snipers can't counter at range 1 without a Mini Bow.

### Route mechanics and your builds

- **Battalions and gambits.** Theodora recruits battalions at town nodes (Recruit Soldiers) and hands them to her units.
  - Only infantry can hold one: mounted units of every kind never can.
  - A unit takes only a battalion at or below its Authority rank.
  - Charm raises gambit power.
  - Each gambit needs an exact spot relative to its target.
  - Gambits don't work in Clashes (fortunesweave.co.uk and fortunesweave.online, community-reported).
  - Battalion stats and gambit numbers are not documented.
- **Who carries them.** Infantry or armored units, highest Cha first: Theodora, Ultand (Cha 50), Ninae, Sirocco, Sofia, Bonaventure, then Tobias, Loretta, Lilian, Simon.
  - Lysander, Alexandra (once she flies) and Nydine are mounted or fly and can't carry one.
  - Novice Hunters (Ch 4) go on Theodora.
- **Authority.** Arena Heavy, Mage and Mounted Training all raise it, so the carriers gain it from the seats they already take.
  - Tactics Manuals go to Theodora first, then Sirocco.
  - Manual source: the Ch 7 Transfer Battalions tutorial.
  - Manual source: item shops in Breston (x2), Regia, Callianeira Port and Grand Aragon.
  - Manual source: 100 troop points per manual from surplus battalions at the Transport Chief (community-reported).
  - Authority arts in the community sheet: Theodora's Attack Formation (C) and Frontline Gambit (S: Atk +5 after an offensive gambit), Sofia's Healing Order (B), Tobias's Power-Arts Order (A).
- **Renown.** Renown 5 (usually Ch 7), 7 (late Ch 8) and 9 (Ch 10 to 11) improve Recruit Soldiers and raise how many battalions you can field?
  - The Send Supplies quests no longer gate any exam here (Guardian and Dragoon carry in), but their Renown feeds these perks.
- **Solomon Island.** Skip it: many turns, no special items. Spend those turns on Arena weeks and the Ch 11 paralogues.

### Spare Turns (second route and later)

Quests you finished on an earlier route complete themselves here, so most free times end with Turns to spare. Each chapter has a **Spare Turns** list. Work it top to bottom:

1. **Gear:** gather the materials for the next Vandahl barters. Barters are the cheapest way to the builds' weapons and rings.
2. **Supports:** run the suggested dungeon with this week's meal pairs in the party. Link attacks raise Support between the two linked units, and so does the campfire meal?
3. **Inn Auto-Activities:** hand the rest of the Turns to the Inn for gold, EXP and materials.

- Dungeon enemies come back every chapter; chests open once.
- Meals, gifts, the Theater and the Thermae are weekly slots, not Turns, so do them every week anyway.

## Supports: gifts and meals, week by week

### How it works (confirmed rules)

- Meals are at the **Inn in Dagsion**.
- You get **3 meals a week**: the menu has a few dishes, and each can be ordered once a week. The menu resets on **Sunday**.
- **3 seats per meal**, and every pair at the table gains Support.
- Recruit Support only counts **with Theodora**, so the setup is always **Theodora + 2 targets**. A meal without Theodora wastes the recruit Support.
- **Price:** 300G per meal, **150G on Sunday**. Hold all three meals on Sunday. Meals cost no Turn.
- **People who haven't joined can come to meals** once you've given them one gift. That's how you build recruit Support before they join.

**Picking a dish**

- **Pick a dish all three like.** Each diner shows an icon on the dish: two notes means loves, one note means likes, an angry icon means dislikes.
- If all three at least like it, you get a **Perfect** meal (bigger gain).
- A liked dish also restores 2 Motivation instead of 1, so do the Arena and Temple first, then eat.
- **Dish data:** `dishes.json` has Theodora and every target (Field Kit › Meals › Which dish). Theodora likes Goat Roast, Garlic Seared Scallops, Salt-Crusted Ham, Fish-Sauce Soup, Sand Boar and Beans, Cheese-Coated Crayfish and Charred Fish Grill, and dislikes Sweet-and-Sour Stick Bugs; only those seven can be Perfect.
- Two hints from that data:
  - Sofia likes or loves every recorded dish, so she never breaks a Perfect when she fills a seat.

**Gifts and finding people**

- **Gifts:** one per character per week, no Turn cost.
- A **loved** gift (two gold arrows) gives about double.
- **The first gift only unlocks the bond** (Game8), so make it the cheapest liked item and save good gifts for later weeks.
- **Finding people:** open **Map > People**. It shows who is in Dagsion right now and your Support with them, and it lets you teleport to them.
- Sources don't say which chapter most targets first show up there, so check it every week. Alexandra is there from 5/29.
- **Speed guide:** one player reached Support 2 in a single Sunday of three Perfect meals. Support 3 takes several weeks, so start early.
